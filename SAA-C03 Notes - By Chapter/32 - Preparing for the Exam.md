## 02 - State of Learning Checkpoint
(src: 32/02-State of Learning Checkpoint - AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate)

- Review the **exam guide** (17 pages) and the appendix of in-scope services/concepts; services listed there but not taught are unlikely to matter (usually about one question out of 65).
- Recommended: 1 year of hands-on experience (the course provides enough practice).
- Question types: **multiple choice** (1 correct, 3 incorrect) and **multiple response** (2+ correct out of 5). **Answer every question** (no penalty).
- **65 questions, 15 unscored** (unknown to you) -> 50 scored. Score **100-1000**, passing **720**.
- Result shows domain-level pass/not pass, not a score per domain, and never which answers were right.

| Domain | Weight |
|---|---|
| Design secure architectures | 30% |
| Design resilient architectures | 26% |
| Design high-performing architectures | 24% |
| Design cost-optimized architectures | 20% |

> Confirmed: 65 questions, 130 minutes, 720 pass mark, $150 and the domain weights match AWS's exam guide ([SAA-C03 exam guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-associate-03/solutions-architect-associate-03.html)); the 15 unscored questions are confirmed only by secondary sources.

## 03 - Exam Tips
(src: 32/03-Exam Tips - AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate)

- Practice makes perfect; do not rush; repeat the course if overwhelmed.
- Questions are mostly **scenario-based**: **eliminate** wrong answers first. Few trick questions; do not overthink - an answer that is feasible but too complicated is probably wrong.
- Unsure? Answer then **flag for review**.
- Skim whitepapers: **Architecting for the Cloud: AWS Best Practices**, **AWS Well-Architected Framework**, **AWS Disaster Recovery**.
- Read **service FAQs** (e.g. `aws.amazon.com/vpc/faqs`) - many exam questions come from them.
- Join the community (course Q&A, forums, blogs, meetups, re:Invent videos); do the practice test.

## 05 - Exam Walkthrough and Signup
(src: 32/05-Exam Walkthrough and Signup)

### Hands-on steps
1. Go to `aws.training/certification` -> Sign In; create an **AWS Builder ID** if needed.
2. Go to your account (certification portal) -> Exam Registration -> schedule an exam (authorize first if shown).
3. Redirected to **Pearson VUE**: choose **test center** or **online with OnVUE**; select language, location/time zone, date and time, then pay (add voucher code if any).
4. Online: needs a webcam and good internet; **run the system test well before the exam**; clear desk; show your room; bring ID; **check in about 30 minutes early**.
5. On exam day: Home -> Dashboard -> Manage my Pearson VUE account -> Launch exam.

## 08 - How does the exam work?
(src: 32/08-How does the exam work-)

- Register online, pay **$150**, bring one ID. No notes, pen or speaking; room scan if at home.
- **65 questions in 130 minutes**; flag feature; cannot change answers after submission.
- Pass: **720/1000**; result within **5 days** (usually sooner) by email, downloadable from the certification site; never see which answers were wrong.
- If you fail: retake after **14 days**.
